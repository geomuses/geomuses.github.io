# Human Refrigerators Keep People Cool in Japan

As extreme heat waves become more common around the world, there are days that are so hot, you might wish you could just climb inside a refrigerator! Well, now you can safely do just that — thanks to something called the Do Hiemon Box.

This "personal cooling box" is very easy to use. If you get too hot, you just get inside it, sit down, and the box will cool your whole body! Trusco Nakayama, the company that sells the Do Hiemon Box, says it is "like a human refrigerator."

The inside of the box is kept at 15 degrees Celsius, so it's already nice and cool when somebody gets inside. And then when they sit down, cooler air at 5 degrees Celsius is blown from the back and top of the box.

But this cool air will automatically stop blowing after 20 minutes so people don't get too cool!

The idea for the box was inspired by the frozen food vending machines that are quite common in Japan.

As Japan's summers get warmer, heatstroke is becoming more of a danger. So Trusco Nakayama hopes the box will help people at places where heatstroke is likely, such as construction sites, factories and outdoor events.

The company says the box can be used indoors and outdoors, uses about half the power of a conventional spot air conditioner, and running it costs only about 16 yen (around 10 US cents) an hour. It's also on wheels, so it can be easily moved around.

The Do Hiemon Box went on sale in April for 1.5 million yen (around $9,200), but they are also available to rent at 300,000 yen ($1,800) a month. They can already be seen in some public spaces, like Maebashi City Hall in Gunma prefecture.
