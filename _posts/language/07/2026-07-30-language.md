# 74% of US Consumers Have Used AI to Shop

What's the first thing you do before deciding whether or not to buy something? Do you compare prices, read reviews, or look at different brands?

Well, according to a report by business technology company CI&T, more and more people are now using AI to help with those things before they decide what to buy.

The company surveyed over 1,000 US consumers and found that 74% have already used an AI agent while shopping, and 78% believe AI makes them a smarter shopper.

Fifty-five percent also said that using AI makes them shop more!

Melissa Minkow of CI&T said this might be because the way we shop has changed over the years.

In the past, many people found a product first, then researched it, before finally deciding whether or not to buy it. But now people are more likely to do their research first and then discover products.

Minkow said that AI has become the first place that many people go to begin that research.

But they're using it for more than just getting started.

According to the report, 55% of people use AI to compare different brands, while another 55% use it to find the lowest prices. Nearly half also use AI to check where specific products are available.

And as more shoppers use AI, retailers are even adding it to their own services. For example, US retail giant Walmart has its own AI chatbot called Sparky to help customers find products and get recommendations.
