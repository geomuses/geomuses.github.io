# Who Are Japan's Biggest-Spending Tourists?

Foreign tourists spent more money in Japan between April and June 2026 than during the same period last year, even though fewer people visited the country.

According to a new survey by the Japan Tourism Agency, foreign tourists spent about 2.51 trillion yen — or about $15.3 billion — during the three-month period.

Although visitor numbers fell by 2.7% from the same period in 2025, the average tourist spent 0.2% more.

But one country stood out from the rest — tourists from Mexico spent the most money per person. On average, each visitor spent almost 515,000 yen, or about $3,150, during their stay.

That was more than double the overall average. Mexican tourists were also the only ones who spent more than 500,000 yen each.

However, Mexican visitors did not spend the most overall. Only about 54,000 people visited Japan from Mexico during the three-month period, so the total spent was 27.9 billion yen, or about $170 million.

Meanwhile, visitors from the US spent 384.8 billion yen, or about $2.35 billion in total — the highest of any country. Taiwan was second, while China fell to third after its spending decreased by almost half compared with the year before.

The survey also showed what tourists spent their money on.

Hotels were the biggest expense for visitors, making up 37% of all spending. Shopping came next at 27%, followed by food and drinks at 22%.
